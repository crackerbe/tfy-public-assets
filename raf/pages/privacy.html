Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Roofer Ad Funnels makes Facebook ads and landing pages for roofing companies. It is run by ChadCreatives, Meridian, Idaho. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@rooferadfunnels.com.

What we collect

  • Free ads form: your website or Facebook page and your email.
  • When you buy: your name, company, email and billing details. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your project details: what you send us to build your ads and page, such as your logo, colors, photos, reviews, phone number, service area, offers and guarantees, and where you want quote requests sent.
  • Quote requests on pages we host: when a homeowner fills in the form on a landing page we host for you, we receive what they enter (such as name, address, ZIP and phone) so we can send it to you.
  • Site use: basic details like pages visited, device, browser and the link that brought you here, collected with cookies and similar tools.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

How we use it

  • To make and deliver your ads and landing page.
  • To send quote requests from your page to you.
  • To take payment and send receipts and account emails.
  • To answer your questions and improve our work.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the service, and only what they need:

  • Stripe, for payments.
  • LanderLab, which hosts landing pages and their forms.
  • Email and file tools we use to receive your files and send your work.
  • Meta and analytics tools, which measure visits and the ads we run for Roofer Ad Funnels itself.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

Cookies and advertising

We may use the Meta Pixel and similar tools to see which of our own ads bring visitors and to show our ads to people who visited this site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and change the ads you see in your Facebook ad preferences or at aboutads.info. Our site does not respond to Do Not Track signals.

Homeowner information on your page

When we host your landing page, the quote requests belong to you. We pass them to you and keep a copy only as long as we need it to run the page. You are responsible for how you use and contact the homeowners who reach out.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we work together and as long as needed for records and the law. Ask us and we will delete what we don't have to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing emails, at any time. Email support@rooferadfunnels.com with the subject "Privacy request". Depending on your state, you may have more rights, and we will honor them.

Children

This is a service for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children's information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.

Contact
